LittleMove Stock Photo Placeholders
=====================================

The SVG files in this directory are placeholder illustrations used until real
lifestyle photos are uploaded via the admin panel.

To replace them, upload your own photos in:
  Admin → Website → Gallery & media  (for the #LittleMoveKids grid)
  Admin → Website → Slides / Banners  (for the hero)

Suggested free-license stock photos (Unsplash — free for commercial use):
  https://unsplash.com/collections/9463506/wooden-sensory-toys
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/toddler-wooden-toys
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/sensory-play-kids
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/baby-learning-toys
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/children-playing-colorful

Suggested free-license stock photos (Pexels — free for commercial use):
  https://www.pexels.com/search/wooden%20toys%20children/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/baby%20sensory%20play/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/toddler%20learning/

Guidelines for hero photos:
  - Minimum size: 1920×1080 px
  - Bright, cheerful, natural light
  - Children's faces ideally not shown (parental consent issue)
  - Horizontal format for desktop; upload a separate square crop as Mobile image

Guidelines for gallery photos (#LittleMoveKids):
  - Customer photos shared with permission
  - Square or portrait format
  - Bright and colourful
