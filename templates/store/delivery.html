Delivery and returns

Delivery

We deliver all over Pakistan by courier. Orders in {{ shop.city }} usually arrive in 1–2 days, other cities in 3–5 working days.

Delivery costs {{ shop.delivery_fee|rs }} per order{% if shop.free_delivery_over %}, and is free when your order is {{ shop.free_delivery_over|rs }} or more{% endif %}.

Before packing, we confirm your order and address on WhatsApp. If we can't reach you within 2 days, the order is cancelled.

Payment

Pay cash to the rider when the parcel arrives{% if shop.jazzcash_number or shop.easypaisa_number %}, or send the amount in advance by{% if shop.jazzcash_number %} JazzCash{% endif %}{% if shop.jazzcash_number and shop.easypaisa_number %} or{% endif %}{% if shop.easypaisa_number %} EasyPaisa{% endif %} and enter the transaction ID at checkout{% endif %}.

Returns and replacements

Please check the parcel when it arrives. If a toy is damaged, broken or not what you ordered, send us a photo on WhatsApp within 3 days and we'll replace it at no cost.

Unused toys in their original packing can be returned within 7 days. The return delivery charge is paid by the customer, and we refund the toy's price once we receive it.

For hygiene reasons, opened play dough, slime and mouthing toys can't be returned unless they arrived faulty.
