How do I pay?

Pay cash to the rider when your parcel arrives. You can also pay in advance by {% if shop.jazzcash_number %}JazzCash{% endif %}{% if shop.jazzcash_number and shop.easypaisa_number %} or {% endif %}{% if shop.easypaisa_number %}EasyPaisa{% endif %} and enter the transaction ID at checkout.

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

{{ shop.city }} orders usually arrive in 1–2 days, other cities in 3–5 working days. Delivery is {{ shop.delivery_fee|rs }}{% if shop.free_delivery_over %}, and free on orders of {{ shop.free_delivery_over|rs }} or more{% endif %}.

What if a toy arrives damaged?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 3 days and we'll replace it at no cost. Unused toys in their original packing can be returned within 7 days.

How do I know a toy suits my child's age?

Every toy shows the ages it suits and the skills it helps with. If you're unsure, message us your child's age and we'll suggest options.

Are these the toys used in therapy?

Many are. LittleMove started at Wellness Rehabilitation Clinic in {{ shop.city }}, and toys marked "Therapist pick" are ones our therapists recommend for practice at home.

Do I need an account to order?

No. Add toys to your cart, fill in your name, number and address, and we'll confirm your order on WhatsApp.
